# Big Timer — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

Big Timer does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No network access: the app never sends data off your device.
- Timer settings are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
- Notifications are scheduled locally on your device, only so the alarm can ring when the app is in the background.
